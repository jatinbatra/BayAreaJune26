🔒 Private · Leadership Review prep (just for you, tap to open)

Discreet on purpose. Nobody needs to know what Monday morning is, on the surface it's just a "very important senior leadership review." Here's how to walk in calm and sharp.

The night before

  • Pick your 6 strongest stories and nothing more, one per theme.
  • Re-read your own notes once; don't cram new material.
  • Lay out clothes, test wifi/camera, charge everything.
  • Sleep > one more rehearsal. Be done by ~11p.

Themes to have ready

  • Led through ambiguity / set a vision
  • Hard call with incomplete data
  • Conflict, peer or up the chain
  • Scaled a team / grew people
  • A real failure + what changed after
  • Influence without authority

STAR, tight

S/T15 sec of context, stakes & your role A60–90 sec, what YOU did, the tradeoffs, the why RNumbers + what you'd repeat / change

At senior level they're listening for judgment, ownership, and scope, not activity. Say "I decided," not "we tried."

Morning of

  • Light food, water, a short walk to burn nerves.
  • Two questions ready to ask them.
  • Slow your first answer down by 20%.
  • It's a conversation between peers, not a test.
